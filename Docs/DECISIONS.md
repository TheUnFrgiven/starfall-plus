# Starfall+ active direction

Confirmed by the owner on 17 November 2026: the current astronaut/elemental-meteor Unity game is the new base, named **Starfall+**. This decision supersedes the older Godot migration direction for future development. Historical prototypes remain available as references.

- Keep Unity/C# and the latest pixel-art assets as the main art direction.
- A player-controlled astronaut explores a spaceship during the day and automatically attacks incoming swarms at night. Movement remains direct; do not restore tap-to-next-objective routing.
- Progress and upgrades carry between nights. Health persists with at most 10 dawn healing; Shield refills before a night. Scaling should build toward a crowded, rewarding power fantasy without section resets.
- Use very simple labels and readable, restrained menus. The floating clickable Bot supplies initial tutorial guidance and contextual help.
- Collection holds continuous-scroll staged Unlockables, recolors, Statistics and Tools. Claims reveal the next related milestone.
- Maths is child-friendly, untimed and supported visually. Choose difficulty first. Fixed-target topics must retain their promised target. After a worked example, ask a fresh similar question; never treat viewing a solution as independent success or as an upgrade purchase.
- Accounts are adult-managed; learners use nicknames. Keep class comparisons private and optional. Separate device-reported practice from server-graded competitive evidence.
- All six existing upgrades remain for review. Proposals to merge Drones into Blaster, change Repair, simplify Tower choices or replace Collector are not approvals. Likewise, budget-based maths and upgrade-linked difficulty need the owner's decision.

Canonical implementation: this repository root. Canonical scene: `Assets/Scenes/StarfallPlus.unity`. Current scope and limitations: `PROJECT_STATUS.md`.
